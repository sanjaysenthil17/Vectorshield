🎤 Part 1: Slide Presentation Script (Minutes 0:00 – 6:30)

0:00 - 2:00 Speaker 1: Vignesh V S (Lead Architect)

SHOW SLIDE 1 (Title) ➔ SLIDE 2 (Problem) ➔ SLIDE 3 (Objectives) ➔ SLIDE 4 & 5 (Topology)
Slide 1 (Title): "Good morning, respected external examiners, faculty members, and peers. I am Vignesh V S, lead architect of AIDS Team 14. Along with Karthick Ruban, Sanjay Senthil, and Hari Prakash, guided by Dr. Lekshmi CR, we present Vector-Shield: a real-time neuro-symbolic security middleware protecting Agentic RAG from Indirect Prompt Injections."

Slide 2 (The Enterprise Threat): "Modern AI agents use RAG to execute financial transfers and manage cloud systems. However, they possess a fatal flaw: Indirect Prompt Injections hidden inside vendor PDFs, emails, or invoices. When retrieved, the LLM treats attacker instructions as system commands. Cloud guardrails like Llama Guard add 800 to 2,500ms latency and per-token costs. Vector-Shield solves this in the pre-retrieval vector space."

Slide 4 & 5 (Architecture): "Our dual-stage gateway embeds queries into 384 dimensions using MiniLM. Stage 1's symbolic Local Outlier Factor filters safe vectors in 1.8ms or quarantines anomalies. Borderline payloads route to Stage 2 ModernBERT on our GPU cluster. If compromised, LangGraph purges the poison and rewinds agent state."

2:00 - 3:30 Speaker 2: Karthick Ruban T (Threat Simulation & Math)

SHOW SLIDE 6 (Stage 1 Math) ➔ SLIDE 7 (Decision Boundaries) ➔ SLIDE 8 (Corpus)
Slide 6 (Mathematical Formulation): "Stage 1 computes Local Outlier Factor over an adaptive 16-dimensional PCA projection, scaled by the cosine distance to the corporate centroid: LOF_scaled = LOF_k(p) * (d_cos / tau)^1.2. This penalizes semantic drift away from core enterprise knowledge."

Slide 7 & 8 (Threat Corpus): "Our three-tier soft-margin classifies vectors into SAFE (≤1.30), BORDERLINE (1.30-2.50), and QUARANTINE (≥2.50). We evaluated across a scaled 175-sample corpus: 100 genuine enterprise policies (SWIFT banking, AWS IAM, GDPR) and 75 real prompt injections from Hugging Face and NVIDIA Garak."

3:30 - 5:00 Speaker 3: Sanjay Senthil (Deep Learning & GPU Training)

SHOW SLIDE 9 (ModernBERT) ➔ SLIDE 10 (A100 Training) ➔ SLIDE 11 (Validation Report)
Slide 9 & 10 (ModernBERT & GPU Training): "For Stage 2, we utilized answerdotai/ModernBERT-base (149M params, 8,192 token window, FlashAttention-2). We fine-tuned the model on an NVIDIA A100 80GB GPU with mixed-precision FP16. Cross-entropy loss converged from 0.5296 to 0.0625 across 3 epochs."

Slide 11 (Validation & Latency): "Stage 2 achieved 100% precision, recall, and F1-score on holdout sets. Forward-pass latency on the A100 is just 12 to 22 milliseconds, ensuring total inspection time remains under 25ms."

5:00 - 6:30 Speaker 4: Hari Prakash V (LangGraph & Systems)

SHOW SLIDE 12 (Self-Healing) ➔ SLIDE 13 (Benchmarks) ➔ SLIDE 14 (Threat Scenarios)
Slide 12 (LangGraph Self-Healing): "Existing guardrails abort conversations with hard refusals. Vector-Shield introduces stateful self-healing via LangGraph. When poison is caught, the graph blacklists the vector ID, purges the chunk, and retrieves the next clean rank."

Slide 13 (Benchmark Telemetry): "Vector-Shield achieves a 460x speedup over Llama Guard (1.82ms vs 850ms) with zero token consumption and 100% quarantine recall. We will now demonstrate this live on our deployed security cockpit."
