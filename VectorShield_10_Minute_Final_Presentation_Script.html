🎤 Part 1: Slide Presentation Script (Minutes 0:00 – 6:30)

0:00 - 2:00 Speaker 1: The Critical Threat, Objectives & Topology

PROJECT: Slide 1 (Title) → Slide 2 (RAG Vulnerability) → Slide 3 (Objectives) → Slides 4-5 (Topology)
Slide 1 (Title): "Respected external examiners, faculty members, and peers. Welcome to our final degree review presentation. Today, our team presents Vector-Shield: a real-time, neuro-symbolic security middleware engineered to defend autonomous Agentic RAG systems against Indirect Prompt Injections and vector data poisoning."

Slide 2 (The Enterprise Threat): "In enterprise environments, AI agents execute autonomous actions like domestic wire transfers and database queries. However, vector databases retrieve data purely through mathematical proximity (Cosine similarity) with zero awareness of payload safety. Attackers exploit this via Indirect Prompt Injections (IPI) hidden in third-party invoices or PDFs. Existing guardrails like Llama Guard add massive latencies of 800 to 2,500ms and per-token costs. Vector-Shield operates directly in the pre-retrieval vector representation space."

Slide 4 & 5 (Architecture): "Our dual-stage gateway embeds queries into 384 dimensions using MiniLM. Stage 1's symbolic LOF filters safe vectors (≤1.30) in 1.8ms or quarantines anomalies (≥2.50). Borderline vectors route to Stage 2 ModernBERT on our GPU cluster. If compromised, LangGraph purges the poison and rewinds agent state."

2:00 - 3:30 Speaker 2: Stage 1 Mathematical Formulation & Threat Corpus

PROJECT: Slide 6 (Stage 1 Math) → Slide 7 (Decision Boundaries) → Slide 8 (Threat Corpus)
Slide 6 (Mathematical Formulation): "Stage 1 computes Local Outlier Factor over an adaptive 16-dimensional PCA projection, scaled by the cosine distance to the enterprise centroid: LOF_scaled = LOF_k(p) * (d_cos / tau)^1.2. This penalizes semantic drift away from verified knowledge clusters."

Slide 7 & 8 (Threat Corpus): "Our three-tier soft-margin classifies vectors into SAFE (≤1.30), BORDERLINE (1.30-2.50), and QUARANTINE (≥2.50). We evaluated across a scaled 175-sample corpus: 100 genuine enterprise policies (SWIFT banking, AWS IAM, GDPR) and 75 real prompt injections from Hugging Face and NVIDIA Garak."

3:30 - 5:00 Speaker 3: Stage 2 ModernBERT Architecture & A100 GPU Training

PROJECT: Slide 9 (ModernBERT) → Slide 10 (A100 GPU Training) → Slide 11 (Validation Report)
Slide 9 & 10 (ModernBERT & GPU Training): "For Stage 2, we integrated answerdotai/ModernBERT-base (149M params, 8,192 token window, FlashAttention-2). We fine-tuned the model on an NVIDIA A100 80GB GPU with mixed-precision FP16. Cross-entropy loss converged from 0.5296 to 0.0625 across 3 epochs, achieving 100% precision, recall, and F1-score."

Slide 11 (Validation & Latency): "Stage 2 forward-pass latency on the A100 is just 12 to 22 milliseconds, ensuring total inspection latency remains well below 25ms—substantially faster than traditional LLM guardrails."

5:00 - 6:30 Speaker 4: LangGraph Self-Healing & Benchmark Comparison

PROJECT: Slide 12 (Self-Healing) → Slide 13 (Benchmarks) → Slide 14 (Threat Scenarios)
Slide 12 (LangGraph Self-Healing): "Existing guardrails abort conversations with hard refusals. Vector-Shield introduces stateful self-healing via LangGraph. When poison is caught, the graph blacklists the vector ID, purges the chunk, and retrieves the next clean rank."

Slide 13 (Benchmark Telemetry): "Vector-Shield achieves a 600x speedup over Llama Guard (1.82ms vs 1,250ms) with zero token consumption and 100% quarantine recall. We will now demonstrate this live on our deployed security cockpit."
